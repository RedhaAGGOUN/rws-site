WHAT

Oil and gas production simulator

Understand a field. Then decide.

RWS is the oil and gas simulator for students and managers: the reservoir, the wells and the surface in one program. The program speaks English, French and Arabic.

LEVELS

Four levels

From the first lesson to the investment decision. Pick a level to see its page.

1

Learn

  • 105 lessons in 13 chapters, each with a goal, exercises and questions.
  • An encyclopedia of 91 terms; a sheet of 66 formulas.
  • Pictures you move yourself: the water front, the inflow of a well, the decline.
  • A wrong answer leads to the lesson that explains it.
2

Practise

  • 16 examples solved step by step: the problem, the method, what to notice.
  • Two guided field studies, one step at a time.
  • Norne: nine years of recorded production, matched by hand and then automatically.
3

Build

  • Your own model: grid, rock, fluids, wells, surface network.
  • Three levels of run: the reservoir alone, with its wells, or with the surface.
  • The result explains itself, from its own numbers.
4

Decide

  • The field in one page: size, production, what is left, pressure, water, wells.
  • Each action in money: cost, value, payback, break-even price.
  • A one-page decision brief to print or send.
