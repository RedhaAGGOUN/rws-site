WHO

Who we are

The startup behind RWS, and where to find us.

Vision

The whole field in one view, from the pore to the profit.

Mission

We make the experts understood: by the students who will join them, and by the managers who turn their work into profit.

Our values

For the people who work on RWS, and for those who work with it.

  1. 1

    The big picture

    In a noisy world, we keep the whole field in view.

    For the team

    We ask what a change does to the reservoir, the wells, the surface and the money before we make it.

    For our partners

    Answers that hold together from end to end.

  2. 2

    Simplicity

    If it cannot be explained simply, it is not finished.

    For the team

    We remove before we add, and we test every screen on someone who has never seen it.

    For our partners

    A tool your people can use on the first day.

  3. 3

    The timely decision

    An answer matters only if it comes in time to decide.

    For the team

    We finish every piece of work with what it means and what to do next.

    For our partners

    Results you can take to a meeting and decide on.

  4. 4

    With the giants, not against them

    We grow by making others stronger.

    For the team

    We respect the tools of the large companies, and we build what leads people toward them.

    For our partners

    A partner who brings you users and clients, never a rival.

  5. 5

    Generosity

    We give knowledge before we ask for anything.

    For the team

    We share what we know, credit the work of others, and say plainly what we have not verified.

    For our partners

    Free access for students, open source, and honest limits.
