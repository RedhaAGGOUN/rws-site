On Norne, three operations are valued with two models that both reproduce the past. Net gain after tax, in million dollars.

New producer80 M$ invested
Shut the wettest well2 M$ invested
30 % more water20 M$ invested
Automatic matchMatch by hand
  1. Drill the new producer.Both models agree. It pays from 14 $ a barrel.
  2. Shut the wettest well now.Two million invested, a gain in both models.
  3. Water: measure before committing 20 M$.The models differ by almost 90 M$. One pressure measurement tells them apart.

Computed by RWS, oil at 70 $ a barrel, tax 78 % (Norway, applied as one rate). Costs and chances of success are assumptions of the study. A teaching model: this is not advice about the real field.
