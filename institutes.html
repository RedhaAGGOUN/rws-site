No internet needed

Teacher and class

The teacher creates an assignment; each student does it and saves a result file; the teacher collects and marks the results of the class.

SEMESTER

A semester with RWS

A suggestion over twelve weeks, to adapt to your course. It follows the four levels, from the first lesson to the decision brief.

  1. Weeks 1 to 4

    LearnLEVEL 1

    The lessons, chapter by chapter, with their exercises and questions. The encyclopedia and the formula sheet stay open beside them.

  2. Weeks 5 to 7

    PractiseLEVEL 2

    The 16 worked examples: the problem, the method, what to notice. Then a first guided field study, one step at a time.

  3. Weeks 8 and 9

    The Norne fieldLEVEL 2

    Each group matches the model to nine years of recorded production, first by hand, then automatically, and compares the two.

  4. Weeks 10 and 11

    BuildLEVEL 3

    Students build their own model: grid, rock, fluids, wells, surface network. They run it alone, with its wells, then with the surface.

  5. Week 12

    DecideLEVEL 4

    Each group values its actions (cost, value, payback, break-even price) and hands in a one-page decision brief. That is the work to mark.

For companies: managers who no longer practise simulation follow a guided field study and finish with the decision brief. Training on request.
